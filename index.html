Multimedia Portfolio

In this class, I have significantly advanced my technical skills in Adobe Photoshop by transforming raw school event imagery into complex, stylized visual designs. Through these projects, I mastered advanced layering techniques, intricate grid-based masking, and intentional color grading to create dynamic composite action shots. I also gained experience working with asset isolation, texturing, and blend modes to blend vintage aesthetic styles with modern graphic layouts, alongside using high-contrast filtering to build high-energy, cinematic sports designs.

Building on my current foundation, I am now comfortable utilizing Photoshop's selection tools, layer styles, and compositing workflows to manipulate images and arrange clean marketing layouts. My primary goal for the remainder of this class is to move beyond these core fundamentals and dive deeper into advanced photo restoration, non-destructive smart object workflows, and complex digital painting. By mastering these advanced tools, I plan to bridge the gap between photo editing and true digital art, allowing me to bring any creative vision to life from scratch.
